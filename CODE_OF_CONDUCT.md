# Code of Conduct

We want an open, respectful community where people can contribute regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussion and review.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about work, without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, differing perspectives, and project decisions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help improve the shared space.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Responses should be proportionate, explain the behavior at issue, and protect the privacy of affected people. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

To report misconduct, request a private communication channel from the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/AliAdilQ) without posting sensitive details publicly. Reports should be handled fairly and confidentially.

This concise policy follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
